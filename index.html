the studio

A soft place to land

Tucked away from the noise, Julias Yoga Studio is a light-filled room with linen cushions, warm wood, dried flowers and the quiet scent of palo santo. There are no crowded classes, no mirrors and no comparison here. It's just you, your practice, and someone who truly sees you.

Only you in the room

Every session is private, so the practice is shaped completely around your body, your story and how you feel today.

Body, mind & spirit

We blend mindful movement, therapeutic tools, breathwork and stillness to support all of you.

Unhurried & sacred

Tea before, a moment of quiet after. Time slows down here, and so does your nervous system.

How a session unfolds

  1. Arrive

    A cup of herbal tea and a gentle check-in. How are you, really?

  2. Move & breathe

    A practice tailored to today. It can be grounding, opening, soothing or energising.

  3. Rest

    Deep relaxation, often with sound, a soft blanket and a guided meditation.

  4. Integrate

    A few tender words, a small home ritual, and you leave feeling lighter.

meet your teacher

Hi, I'm Julia

My own yoga journey began on a tired, anxious evening more than fifteen years ago, when a single long exhale reminded me that I had a home inside myself. I've been gently walking back there ever since.

Today, I hold space for people who want to feel safe in their bodies again: people moving through pain, burnout, big life transitions, or simply a quiet longing for more. I combine the precision of yoga therapy with the warmth of an old friend and a deep respect for yoga's spiritual roots.

"You don't need to be flexible, calm or 'spiritual enough'. You just need to show up as you are. The rest, we'll find together."

with love, Julia

why people stay

The little things that make it magic

  • Held with heart

    Julia remembers your story, your favourite pose and how your week went. You're never just a booking.

  • Gentle, never forced

    No pushing and no "no pain, no gain". Every shape is an invitation, and "no" is always welcome.

  • Trauma-informed

    Choice-based language, consent for touch and a nervous-system-friendly pace create real safety.

  • Rooted in tradition

    Teachings honour yoga's origins, from the Yoga Sutras and Ayurveda to mantra, shared with care rather than as a trend.

  • Science meets soul

    Anatomy and therapeutic knowledge meet intuition and energy work, so you get the best of both worlds.

  • Little rituals

    Seasonal teas, an intention card to take home and a candle lit just for you. It's the kind of detail you'll remember.

4

trips to India to study

  1. 2013

    200h Hatha Yoga Teacher Training

    Rishikesh, India. This is where it all began: asana, pranayama, philosophy and the Bhagavad Gita at the feet of the Himalaya.

  2. 2015

    Yin & Restorative Yoga · 100h

    She learned the art of slowness, fascia and meridians, and how deep rest can be the most healing practice of all.

  3. 2017

    500h Advanced Teacher (E-RYT 500)

    She deepened her study of anatomy, alignment, sequencing and the subtle body, and went on to teach studio classes and retreats.

  4. 2019

    Trauma-Sensitive Yoga · 40h

    She learned to create safe, choice-based spaces for people healing from stress, anxiety and trauma.

  5. 2021

    Certified Yoga Therapist · 800h (C-IAYT)

    This two-year clinical training covered therapeutic assessment, chronic pain, mental health and individual care plans, and it became the heart of her work.

  6. 2023

    Meditation, Sound Healing & Reiki II

    She trained in Vipassana-inspired meditation and singing-bowl therapy and earned her Reiki Level II attunement, bringing more spiritual depth to every session.

  7. today

    Ayurveda & ongoing mentorship

    She is currently studying Ayurvedic lifestyle counselling and continues to work with her own teachers. The learning never stops.
